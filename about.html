MEET THE FOUNDER

Meet Sreeja Vijayan

The person behind The Manomantra.

✦
COUNSELLING PSYCHOLOGIST • ICF ACC CERTIFIED LIFE COACH
PARENTING & GEN Z COACH • FOUNDER, THE MANOMANTRA

Sreeja Vijayan is a Counselling Psychologist, ICF ACC Certified Life Coach, Parenting & Gen Z Coach, and the Founder of The Manomantra.

With over 14 years of experience in counselling, coaching, education and training, she has worked extensively with children, adolescents, young adults, parents, educators and professionals.

Her work integrates evidence-based psychology, coaching, neuroscience, mindfulness and Indian wisdom to help individuals navigate life's transitions with greater awareness, purpose and emotional wellbeing.

Driven by a deep passion for human development, Sreeja founded The Manomantra to create a space where people can discover their strengths, build meaningful relationships and flourish across every stage of life.

MY STORY

My Story.
My Purpose.

✦

For me, The Manomantra is more than a professional practice—it is the expression of a lifelong commitment to helping people understand themselves, navigate transitions and grow into their potential.

My journey across psychology, counselling, coaching, education, career guidance and human development has shown me that people do not need more pressure to change. They need a safe space to become aware, find meaning and take aligned action.

I believe every transition in life carries an invitation— to understand ourselves more deeply and consciously choose who we want to become.

This belief shaped The Manomantra: a human-development ecosystem where children grow with confidence, young people discover direction, parents build connection, women return to themselves, couples strengthen their relationships and professionals flourish with clarity and wellbeing.

My approach is psychologically informed, deeply human and practically grounded. It honours each person's story while helping them build the awareness, skills and inner steadiness required for lasting growth.
