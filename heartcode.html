    GOOD TO KNOW

    Frequently asked questions

    Have a question that is not covered here? Reach out and we will help you understand whether this experience is the right fit.

    How do I find out about the next available batch?

    Open the “Enquire About This Programme” panel to share your details and proceed to the approved payment link. Confirm the current batch dates and availability with THE MANOMANTRA.

    Is the programme online or in person?

    The usual delivery format is shown near the programme title. The exact arrangements, location and schedule can be confirmed during your enquiry.

    How do I register?

    Click “Enquire About This Programme”, complete the registration-interest form and continue to the approved Razorpay payment link once the current intake is confirmed.

    Will the programme be personalised?

    Personalisation depends on the programme. Some experiences are guided journeys, while workshops and organisational programmes may be adapted to the group's needs.
